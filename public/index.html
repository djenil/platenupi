How It Works

Clean engineering, zero middleman custody.

Native NPCI Specification

Barcodes strictly follow the NPCI UPI Common Library standard. Scan seamlessly using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred, or any UPI-enabled banking app.

Zero Escrow or Float

Funds never pass through Platen servers or custodial accounts. Money moves directly from customer to your bank account with zero intermediate clearing delay.

High-DPI Raster Generation

Exports pixel-crisp canvas graphics engineered specifically for 4×6-inch photo printing and desk displays without barcode fuzziness or scanning defects.

Private by Design

No accounts, passwords, or transaction telemetry. Barcode rendering runs entirely in your local browser sandbox or via stateless API requests.
